About LifeCare

A trusted multi-specialty hospital built around you

For two decades, LifeCare has combined clinical excellence with genuine kindness. Our specialists, nurses and support teams work as one to give every patient safe, timely and affordable care.

Our Mission

To deliver ethical, evidence-based and compassionate healthcare that is accessible to every family.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted hospital for clinical quality and patient experience.

  • Experienced specialists across 12 departments, with coordinated multi-disciplinary care.
  • Modern facilities — modular OTs, critical-care ICUs, MRI/CT imaging and automated labs.
  • Transparent & patient-first — clear explanations, insurance help desk and honest estimates.

Outpatient clinics run Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Emergency services, ICU and pharmacy are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
