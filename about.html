LifeCare Multispeciality Hospital was founded on a simple belief: every patient deserves world-class medicine delivered with dignity, warmth and honesty.

Hospital Overview

A complete hospital for the whole family

LifeCare is a multi-specialty hospital offering outpatient consultations, inpatient care, critical care, surgery, diagnostics and rehabilitation — all within one well-connected campus.

Our clinical teams follow standardised, evidence-based protocols and meet regularly to review outcomes, so that the quality of care keeps improving every year.

Inpatient Care

General wards, private rooms and dedicated ICUs.

Outpatient Clinics

Specialist OPDs Monday to Saturday.

Emergency

24×7 trauma and emergency response.

Diagnostics

Imaging, lab and cardiac tests in-house.

Our Journey

Growing with the community we serve

From a small clinic to a full multi-specialty hospital, our growth has always been guided by patients' needs.

    First inpatient wing & operation theatres

    Multi-specialty campus

    Grew to 12 specialty departments with oncology, neurology and gastroenterology.

    Digital-first patient experience

    Online appointment requests, digital reports and teleconsultations.

Hospital Infrastructure

Built for safety, comfort and speed

Our campus is designed so that patients move easily between consultation, diagnostics, treatment and recovery.

  • Modular operation theatres with laminar airflow
  • Medical, surgical, cardiac & neonatal ICUs
  • MRI, CT, digital X-ray, ultrasound & mammography
  • Automated laboratory with home sample collection
  • 24×7 pharmacy, ambulance and blood storage support
  • Wheelchair-friendly access, lifts and patient lounges
